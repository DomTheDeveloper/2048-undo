What does “perfect” mean? Controlled playback follows a legal, verified spawn sequence. It demonstrates what is possible, not a guaranteed win with random tiles. High-score playback is a construction, not a proof of the unrestricted score optimum.

How to play: Use your arrow keys to move the tiles. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one!
